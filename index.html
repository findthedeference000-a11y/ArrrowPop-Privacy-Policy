Privacy Policy for ArrrowPop

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ArrrowPop. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our game.

Information We Collect

ArrrowPop does not require users to create an account. The game stores gameplay information such as high scores, coins, and settings locally on your device using browser local storage.

Third-Party Services

ArrrowPop uses Google Fonts to load its font. When the font is requested, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information.

Data Storage

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. You can remove this information by clearing the app's data or browser storage.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
